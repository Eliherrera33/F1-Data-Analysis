Braking G

Longitudinal deceleration calculated from speed derivative

a = Δv / Δt → G = a / 9.81
🟡

Lateral G

Cornering force from centripetal acceleration

a = v² × κ → G = a / 9.81
🟣

Total G

Combined force magnitude on driver

G_total = √(G_long² + G_lat²)
